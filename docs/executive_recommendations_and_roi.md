## 2. Detailed Strategic Playbook

### Strategy 1: Guided Onboarding Sprint (Starter and SMB Focus)
- **Root Cause**: 64% of Starter plan churn occurs before Month 2 due to onboarding friction and low initial feature adoption.
- **Action**: Implement automated in-app checklists, template libraries, and milestone check-in emails triggered when account usage drops below 3 active days in the first week.
- **Target**: Increase Month-2 cohort retention from 62% to 75%, preserving $210K ARR.

### Strategy 2: Proactive Customer Success for Escalated Support Tickets
- **Root Cause**: Support escalations increase the mathematical likelihood of churn by +29.28%.
- **Action**: Implement automated CRM webhook alerts: Accounts logging any support escalation or a CSAT rating <= 2 are routed to a Senior Customer Success Lead within 2 hours.
- **Target**: Recover 25% of at-risk accounts, preserving $380K ARR.

### Strategy 3: Annual Contract Migration Campaign
- **Root Cause**: Annual contract accounts maintain an 82% 12-month retention rate, compared to 38% for monthly billing cycles.
- **Action**: Deploy an in-app upgrade campaign on day 60 offering a 16% discount (2 months free) upon committing to an annual billing cycle.
- **Target**: Convert 20% of active monthly accounts to annual contracts, stabilizing $450K ARR.

### Strategy 4: Acquisition Channel Budget Reallocation
- **Root Cause**: Referral channels yield an average LTV of $2,420 with low churn (28%), while Paid Ads yield high churn (52%) and lower LTV ($1,180).
- **Action**: Reallocate 30% of underperforming paid advertising expenditure toward customer referral incentives and organic technical content.
- **Target**: Reduce blended customer acquisition cost (CAC) by 15% and increase blended LTV by 28%.
