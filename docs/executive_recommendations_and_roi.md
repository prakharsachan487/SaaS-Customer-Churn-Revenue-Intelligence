## 🎯 Detailed Action Plan

### 1. Guided Onboarding Sprint (Starter / SMB Focus)
- **Insight**: 64% of Starter plan churn occurs before Month 2 due to "Difficult Onboarding / Low Adoption".
- **Action**: Implement interactive in-app checklists, template libraries, and automated 7-day email trigger tips for inactive users.
- **Goal**: Boost Month-2 cohort retention from 62% to 75%.

### 2. Proactive Customer Success for Escalated Tickets
- **Insight**: Support ticket count alone is not the primary churn driver; rather, *ticket escalations* increase churn risk by +29.3%.
- **Action**: Build automated webhook alerts in CRM / Zendesk: Any account logging >= 1 escalation or CSAT <= 2 is assigned a dedicated CSM call within 2 hours.
- **Goal**: Salvage 25% of at-risk accounts, preserving $380K ARR.

### 3. Annual Contract Migration Campaign
- **Insight**: Annual contracts feature an 82% retention rate versus only 38% for monthly billing cycles.
- **Action**: Run an in-app upgrade campaign on day 60 offering "2 Months Free" upon annual commitment.
- **Goal**: Convert 20% of monthly subscribers to annual plans.

### 4. Channel Budget Reallocation
- **Insight**: Referral channels achieve an average LTV of $2,420 and low churn (28%), while Paid Ads show 52% churn and $1,180 LTV.
- **Action**: Reallocate 30% of underperforming paid advertising budget into customer referral rewards and organic SEO content.
- **Goal**: Lower blended CAC by 15% and increase blended LTV by 28%.
