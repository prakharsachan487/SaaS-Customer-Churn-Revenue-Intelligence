# Resume STAR Bullet Points & Interview Talking Points

Use these high-impact, metrics-driven bullet points for your **Resume**, **LinkedIn Project Section**, and **Interview Responses**.

---

## 📄 Bullet Points for Resume (Tailored by Role)

### Option 1: Data Analyst / Business Intelligence Engineer (Recommended)
- **Engineered an End-to-End SaaS Churn & Revenue Intelligence Platform** analyzing 12,500+ customer records and $19M+ ARR using Python, SQL (CTEs, Window Functions), and Power BI.
- **Architected a 3-Page Executive Power BI Suite** with Star-Schema data modeling and 25+ DAX measures (MRR, ARR, NRR, Cohort Retention %, LTV:CAC), enabling real-time risk visibility for C-Suite leadership.
- **Built an Advanced SQL Cohort Matrix (`LAG`, `LEAD`, `ROW_NUMBER`)** tracking Month-0 to Month-12 customer and revenue retention decay curves across customer segments and billing tiers.
- **Developed a Machine Learning Churn Scoring Model** achieving **78.6% accuracy and 87.5% recall**, identifying key churn drivers (Contract Type, Support Escalations, Inactive Days).
- **Delivered 4 Actionable Executive Recommendations** with financial ROI modeling projected to reduce revenue churn by 18% and save **$1.18M in recurring ARR**.

---

### Option 2: Data Science / Machine Learning Analyst
- **Designed a Predictive Customer Retention Pipeline** for enterprise SaaS subscriptions using Python, Scikit-Learn, and SQL staging ETL.
- **Trained and Evaluated ML Churn Risk Classifiers** achieving **87.5% Recall and 79.4% F1-Score**, generating daily proactive risk alerts for 1,899 at-risk accounts ($1.99M ARR in jeopardy).
- **Conducted Hypothesis & A/B Testing** demonstrating annual contracts reduce churn hazard by 2.8x (p < 0.001) and support escalations increase churn likelihood by +29.3%.

---

## 🎙️ How to Explain this in an Interview (The 60-Second Pitch)

> *"In this project, I tackled a critical SaaS business problem: fluctuating MRR and dropping customer retention across 12,500 accounts. 
> 
> I built an end-to-end analytics pipeline: First, I performed ETL and Star Schema modeling in Python and SQL. Then, I wrote advanced SQL queries with CTEs and Window Functions to compute month-over-month cohort retention matrices. 
> 
> Next, I built an interactive 3-page Power BI executive dashboard with 25+ DAX measures covering MRR, NRR, and Customer Lifetime Value. To make the dashboard proactive rather than just descriptive, I trained an ML model with 87.5% recall that flags high-risk accounts before they cancel. 
> 
> Finally, I translated the data into 4 C-level recommendations with ROI projections to protect $1.18M in ARR."*
